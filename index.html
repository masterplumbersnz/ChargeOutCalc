Business Profitability Check

For Plumbing, Gasfitting & Drainlaying businesses - prepared for Master Plumbers, Gasfitters & Drainlayers NZ Inc members.

What this calculator does: Enter what you pay each role to see the true cost of employing them, then set your own margin to calculate a target charge-out rate. Add current rates to see your business-wide position.
What's included, what's excluded, and modelling a mixed trade workforce

This gives a labour charge-out rate only - it excludes materials and material markup, subcontractors, job-specific plant hire, permits/certification fees and call-out charges. This is a planning tool, not financial or tax advice.

Mixed plumbing, gasfitting & drainlaying workforce? Trade is selected on each individual Tradesperson and Certifier row below, not at the top of the page - so you can model your whole team, across all three trades, in one calculation and one business-wide summary.

Assumptions & sources - every rate feeding this calculator

Change any value below and the whole calculator recalculates. These default to published 2026/27 statutory rates and industry estimates. Always confirm your own ACC rate against your latest invoice or MyACC for Business.

Data sources for statutory assumptions: ACC Levy Guidebook 2026/27; Employment New Zealand; Inland Revenue; MBIE. Figures are shown excl. GST unless marked otherwise. This is a planning tool, not financial or tax advice - check figures against your own accounts and ACC invoice before relying on them. No figures provided by this tool are a recommendation, target or pricing benchmark. Target margins start at 0% and no target rate is calculated until the member enters a value greater than 0%. Each business must determine its own prices, margins and commercial strategies independently from its own costs and circumstances. What's deliberately left out: ACC Earner's Levy is excluded - it's deducted from an employee's own pay via PAYE to cover non-work injuries, so it is not a cost to the employer. Less common leave types (bereavement leave, family violence leave, jury service) are also excluded, as their cost is low-incidence and highly business-specific with no reliable industry-wide figure; factor these in yourself if they're relevant to your business.

No information inputted into this tool is stored, retained, monitored, or shared by us. Any data inputted is used solely to perform the calculations you request. "Save my data" and "Open saved file" only read and write files on your own device or a drive/folder you choose - nothing is sent to Master Plumbers or anyone else.
