Charge Out Rate Calculator

For Plumbing, Gasfitting & Drainlaying businesses — prepared for Master Plumbers, Gasfitters & Drainlayers NZ Inc members.

What this calculator does: Punch in what you pay an apprentice, labourer, tradesperson or certifier, and this tool works out every hidden cost of employing them (ACC levies, KiwiSaver, annual leave, public holidays, sick leave, and your own vehicle and overhead costs), then suggests a charge out rate that covers the lot and builds in your margin. Enter the hourly wage in the highlighted field for each role — that's all you need for a suggested rate. All cost inputs and outputs below are excl. GST unless marked otherwise; use the "incl. GST" figure when quoting a customer. Add how many staff you have at each grade and your own current charge-out rate to see the gap across your whole business.
This gives a labour charge-out rate only. It excludes materials and material markup, subcontractors, job-specific plant hire, permits/certification fees and call-out charges — add those separately when pricing a job. This is a planning tool, not financial or tax advice.
Gasfitting adds a certification & risk premium on top of the suggested rate for every role (not stacked into the markup). Plumbing and Drainlaying use the same base rate.
